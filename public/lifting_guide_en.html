What is this tool?

Before anything else, know exactly what this screen builds.

The Lifting Plan Designer builds a data-driven engineering plan for a single crane lift: you place the exact position of the crane, the load, its travel path and the surrounding obstacles on a 2D canvas (to scale, in metres), and the system computes and validates — live — the crane's real capacity from its load chart, utilization percentage, ground bearing pressure, sling angle and tension, and clearance to power lines/obstacles.

ℹ️
No assumed numbers: if the crane has no load chart at the working radius, capacity stays "off chart" and the check fails — the system never substitutes a guessed value.

Anything you draw is a local draft until you press one of the explicit "Save" buttons — nothing reaches the database before that, so feel free to experiment.

Working the canvas (top view)

Once the basic details are saved, the "2D plan (top view)" section becomes active.

The toolbar

Each tool is an object type; pick a tool and click the canvas to drop that object at that point.

ToolWhat it is
🏗️CraneOne click adds a crane and its linked hook together (section 5).
🪝HookUsually created automatically with the crane; you rarely add it by hand.
🧵Sling setThe rigging lines between hook and load.
🔗ShackleConnects the sling set to the load/hook.
➖Spreader beamA load-spreading beam — when enabled, it corrects the sling angle.
🚛TruckA transport vehicle, shown only for its footprint.
🧱Structure / foundationA nearby building or foundation, checked for path interference.
👷WorkerWhere a person is stationed; their clearance to the load path is validated (section 9).
🚧BarrierPhysical fencing around the work area.
⚡Power lineAn overhead conductor at a given voltage; its clearance comes from Acceptance Criteria.
⛔Exclusion zoneAn area the load path must never enter.
⚑Place pointThe load's final destination; only one can exist on the plan (section 7).

Load is drawn with the shape tools (rectangle / circle / free polygon) rather than a single click, because its geometry drives the pick points and centre of gravity (CG) used in section 6.

Once an object is selected

  • The "Object properties" panel opens below the canvas — every numeric field for that object (weight, length, WLL, count, etc.) is edited there.
  • Drag to move, use the handles around the object to rotate and resize.
  • The toolbar also has Zoom / Pan / Grid / Snap-to-grid and Undo / Redo.
👁️
Above the canvas is a view switch: Top view / Elevation / Both. Elevation is fully computed and read-only — all editing happens in the top view.
5

Setting up the crane — a boom + hook assembly

The crane and its hook are one assembly; never drag the hook on its own.

  1. Pick the crane model

    In the "Object properties" panel, set "Crane model (from master data)" to one of the models registered in section 2, so the real load chart is available for the capacity calculation.

  2. Optionally link the machinery registry

    "Linked machinery (from machinery registry)" ties this crane to an actual unit registered in the machinery module (plate number, technical documents, etc.).

  3. Set boom length and boom angle

    These two, together with "Rotation" (the boom's slew bearing), determine the reach and height of the boom tip. The hook always sits exactly there and follows in real time.

  4. Enter weight, pad count and pad area

    These three feed the ground bearing pressure calculation in section 9.

⚠️
The "no load chart" warning: if you see this under the crane fields, the selected model in section 2 has no chart — capacity computes as "off chart" and the safety check stays failed until a model with a chart is selected.
6

Load & rigging

Total load on the hook = load weight + hook + sling set + shackle + (spreader beam, if enabled).

Load

Set its shape (rectangle / circle / polygon), weight, and drag the handles on the load itself to set pick points and centre of gravity (CG). Slings are drawn automatically from the hook to those points.

Hook & sling set

Enter the hook's weight and WLL (rated working load), and the sling set's count/WLL/length in the properties panel. Each leg's tension is computed from the real geometry of the angle (section 9).

Spreader beam — optional

When "enabled", the load hangs from a horizontal beam instead of a direct connection; this usually reduces the sling angle from vertical (better) and lowers the tension per leg.

💡
If the calculations panel warns about "sling angle", the first fix is usually this: enable a spreader beam, or spread the load's pick points further apart.

8

Saving the plan (scene)

Everything you draw on the canvas is local and temporary until you press "Save plan".

The "Save plan" button below the canvas is entirely separate from the "Save" button at the top of the form (section 3): one saves the basic details (number, date, status, …), the other saves the drawing itself (crane, load, rigging, site context). While something on the canvas has changed and isn't saved yet, you'll see "Unsaved plan changes".

💾
Before closing a plan or leaving the page, always make sure both saves (details + drawing) have gone through.

Reading the calculations & safety panel

As soon as the canvas has at least one crane and one load, this panel appears below the plan and updates live.

Three environment fields

"Soil bearing capacity", "Ground safety factor (SF)" and "Load travel height" are manual inputs for this specific plan (not a company-wide setting) and feed directly into the calculations below.

The calculations column

RowWhat it means
Load weight / Rigging weight / Total load on hookSum of load + hook + sling set + shackle + enabled spreader weight.
Working radiusHorizontal distance from crane to load at the current position / simulation phase.
Rated capacity (load chart)Linear interpolation of the crane model's chart at this radius; beyond its range = "off chart".
Utilization(total load ÷ rated capacity) × 100 — green/amber/red per the Acceptance Criteria thresholds.
Sling angle / Max leg tension / Sling WLL per legThe real geometry from hook to the load's pick points.
Ground bearing pressure (worst pad) / AllowableTotal reaction (crane weight + load) on the worst pad, against (soil ÷ SF).

The safety validation column

A list of findings, each with a coloured dot:

LevelMeaning
🟢okWithin limits — no action needed.
🟡warnClose to the threshold — proceed with caution / supervisor review.
🔴failOutside the allowed limit — the plan must not be executed or approved as-is.
🧮
Every threshold (utilization limit, min clearance, sling angle, etc.) comes from Acceptance Criteria (section 2) — nothing in this calculation is hardcoded.
10

Simulating the lift phases

The ▶ button below the canvas plays back the hook's motion from pick to place across six phases.

#PhaseWhat happens
1SetupThe hook sits at the configured boom tip — the same as edit mode.
2PickThe hook lowers onto the load.
3LiftThe hook rises to the load's travel height.
4SlewThe crane rotates in place to line up with the destination bearing.
5TravelThe radius changes until the load reaches the place point.
6PlaceThe hook lowers and the load sets down on the place point.

Click any phase label, or drag the slider beside it, to jump straight to that phase. During simulation, canvas editing is temporarily disabled and the calculations/safety panel updates for that exact moment of motion — so you can review the whole lift path, moment by moment, before it's actually executed.

Status, versioning, duplication & audit trail

An approved plan needs to be traceable: who changed what, and when.

Plan status

Draft In review Approved Rejected Archived

Status changes from the same "Status" field on the plan details form and is recorded on every save.

Freeze revision

Creates an immutable snapshot of the plan's current state and bumps the revision number — use it once a plan has been approved and you want its record locked.

Duplicate

Creates a full copy of the plan (details + canvas) under a new number/revision — for a similar lift on a different project, without starting from a blank canvas.

Audit trail

Every event — created, details updated, plan saved, revision frozen, status changed, duplicated, archived/restored — is recorded with the user and timestamp, visible at the bottom of the edit screen.

  2. Click "New plan", fill in and save the basic details.
